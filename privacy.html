Privacy Policy

Peak Arcade · snailgamedev.github.io/PeakArcade · last updated 2026-05-26

Peak Arcade is a free collection of browser games. We keep things simple and respect your privacy. This page explains what data is used and why.

No accounts, no personal data collected by us

Peak Arcade has no sign-up and no login. We don’t ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and we don’t run our own analytics or tracking servers.

Local storage (stays on your device)

The site and its games save small bits of data in your own browser (via localStorage) — things like your favorites, recently played games, settings, high scores, and in-game progress. This never leaves your device and we can’t see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Peak Arcade is free and supported by ads served through Google AdSense. To do this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

For more on how Google uses data, see the Google Partner Sites policy.

Children

Our games are family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal data and require no accounts, no such information is gathered by us.

Your choices

You can clear local data anytime through your browser settings, and manage ad personalization through the links above. Using the site is entirely optional and free.
