❓ Questions

Are the games on Peak Arcade free?

Yep — 100% free to play. No download, no login, no catch. Just open the page, tap a game, and play.

Do I need to download or install anything?

Nope. Every game runs right in your web browser. If you want, you can also "Add to Home Screen" to install Peak Arcade like an app and even play offline.

Can I play on my phone, tablet, and computer?

Yes — Peak Arcade works on any device with a browser: phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. The games are built to feel great on touch and on keyboard/mouse.

What kinds of games are there?

A growing mix — basketball (Would U Dunk), fighting (Slam City), brain games (BrainBoard), adventure (Catch the Snail 1 & 2), and arcade (Coin Kingdom), with more dropping soon like Chess and Tower Defense.

How is Peak Arcade free? Is it safe?

It's free for you to play and supported by ads, which is what lets us keep building more games. No accounts, nothing to buy. Made with care — and put out to bless your downtime. 🙏
