Every game here is hand-built from scratch — no engines, no fluff, just clean games that run anywhere, instantly. Made with care, built to last, and put out to bless your downtime. Add it to your home screen, play offline, keep comin' back. Peak don't stop. 📈

🤔 Got Questions? We Got You.

Wait… it's actually free? What's the catch?

No catch. 100% free, forever — no download, no login, no credit card, no "free trial" nonsense. You open the page, you tap a game, you play. That's the whole deal. Ads keep the lights on so you never pay a dime. 🙌

Do I have to download or install anything?

Nope — everything runs right in your browser. But if you're feeling it, hit "Add to Home Screen" (or the ⬇ Install button) and Peak Arcade lives on your phone like a real app — and it even works offline on the plane, the subway, wherever. 📲

Phone, tablet, laptop — does it work everywhere?

All of 'em. Built mobile-first and tuned for touch, but it feels just as clean on keyboard + mouse. One arcade, every screen. 📱💻

Do my scores and progress save?

Yes — saved right on your device (in your browser), no account needed. Coins, high scores, unlocks, streaks — all there when you come back. (Heads up: clearing your browser data wipes it, since it only lives on your device.)

What games are in here?

A growing roster — 🏀 basketball chaos (Would U Dunk), 🥊 wrestling (Slam City), 🧠 brain games (BrainBoard), 🐌 adventure (Catch the Snail 1 & 2), 🪙 an 8-in-1 coin arcade (Coin Kingdom), and ✝️ The Word. More always cooking — Chess, Tower Defense, and friends are on deck.

Is it safe for kids / the whole family?

Yes. Built clean on purpose — no gore, no real-money gambling, no sketchy links. The kind of arcade you can hand your little brother without a second thought. 🛡️

What's with the Bible verses? 🙏

We're a faith-built shop, plain and simple. The Word shows up here gentle — a verse on a loading screen, a quiet "✝ The Word" room — never shoved in your face. Come for the games; if a verse meets you on the way, that's the real high score. 💛

Do you track me or sell my data?

No accounts, no personal info collected by us, no data to sell. Ads (via Google) use cookies — you can read exactly how in our Privacy Policy. We kept it honest and short.

Found a bug, or got a game idea?

We'd love it — hit us at ej.hudson1004@gmail.com. Half the stuff in here started as "yo what if…" 🔥
